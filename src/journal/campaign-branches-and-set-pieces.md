The campaign's encounters picked up their own rules. At the roadside, the player opens a stalled convoy's route and crosses before the vehicles leave. In the museum, Davy can fight the orderly or send the raccoon through a staff-room panel to recall him; the later exchange and route reflect that choice.

The service tunnels add a sentry, a maintenance-room detour, a welder, and a steam hazard with warning windows. A final radio confrontation leads to one of two endings, each with its own closing passages. Seven clues and the ending record persist in the browser, while chapter-entry checkpoints offer Continue or New journey after a reload.

These changes were checked with real browser interactions as they were built. The archive keeps the distinction between isolated feature checks and a complete route; both are useful, and they prove different things.

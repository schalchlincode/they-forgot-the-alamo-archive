I asked for Davy to hold the shotgun with both hands like a real shotgun. In this build, his right hand is at the trigger, his left supports the fore-end, and the stock sits closer to his shoulder. During the reload, the support hand moves away so the hinged barrels can open.

A live Edge check covered movement, aiming, firing, and the break-open reload. This image is from that review. The low-resolution view makes the hands difficult to distinguish in a still image.

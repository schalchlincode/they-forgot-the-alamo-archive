The oldest code I have already shows a reload timer and two shells. By the next saved version, the shotgun fires both at once, then opens up so you can put the replacements in one at a time.

I like that little ritual. It gives a very simple gun some personality, and gives the animation plenty of ways to embarrass me. The hands, shells, hinge, barrels, and ammo count all have to agree on what's happening. They did not always agree.

I had to revisit the gun placement once Davy got a proper rig and a new model. This note is about the early reload; it definitely doesn't mean every later version got it right on the first try.

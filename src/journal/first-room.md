This is the earliest version still available in the repository: a small 3D room, Davy Crockett, targets, and a shotgun. The project README describes the premise: David Crockett survives a crash, wakes from a coma convinced he is Davy Crockett, and goes on a rampage.

This is the oldest version that can be verified in the saved project history, not necessarily when work first began. Most of the art is boxes and other simple shapes.

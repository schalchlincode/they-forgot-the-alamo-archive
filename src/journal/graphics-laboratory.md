The render experiments produced a useful pile of paired captures: fog on and off, vertex snap on and off, several dither attempts, and low-resolution before and after. The switches matter because every effect has a cost in readability.

Some tests were probes, not final art direction. The archive keeps them because a game’s appearance is usually found by looking at the ugly intermediate states.

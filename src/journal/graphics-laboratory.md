I ended up with a whole pile of side-by-side captures: fog on and off, snapping on and off, several attempts at dithering, and the low-res setting before and after. Testing one thing at a time made it easier to tell which effect was helping and which one was just making everything muddy.

Some of these are experiments I wouldn't ship as-is. I'm keeping them anyway. A lot of the look comes from trying something, deciding it looks terrible, and figuring out *why* before the next attempt.

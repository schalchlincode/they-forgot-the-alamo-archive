The project moved well beyond its original test room. The campaign follows Davy from a hospital wake-up through a roadside evacuation, an Alamo museum, service tunnels, and a final mission. The same basic controls and shotgun now serve a sequence of spaces with different objectives.

The route adds enemy encounters, clues, a convoy crossing, and a final radio choice. It remains a rough, deliberately compact campaign rather than a claim that every part of the original wish list is finished. The game source now records five chapter starts, seven clues, and two endings.

Earlier entries keep the saved room, weapon, graphics, and character experiments that led here. The intermediate versions matter; a campaign did not spring fully armed from the floorboards.
